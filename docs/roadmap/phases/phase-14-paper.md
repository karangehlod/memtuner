# Phase 14: Paper

## Purpose

Write, review, and finalize the benchmark paper and its reproducibility package.

## Research Question

How should the benchmark methodology, results, limitations, and reproducibility materials be presented to maximize clarity, rigor, and adoption?

## Target Output

Submission-ready benchmark paper package.

## Scope

- Paper outline and claim structure
- Methodology write-up
- Results and analysis write-up
- Limitations and ethics write-up
- Reproducibility appendix and release notes

## Deliverables

- Paper outline
- Draft manuscript
- Figure and table package
- Reproducibility appendix
- Submission checklist

## Workstreams

- Narrative structure
- Methods and experiments writing
- Figure and table integration
- Reproducibility and final review

## Dependencies

- Completed paper experiment artifacts from Phase 13
- Figures and statistical outputs from Phases 9 and 10

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The paper clearly states the benchmark motivation, scope, protocol, datasets, methods, and findings.
2. All empirical claims in the paper trace to benchmark artifacts.
3. Limitations, exclusions, and future work are explicit.
4. The reproducibility appendix is complete and actionable.
5. The paper package is ready for internal review or submission.

## Verification

1. Review the manuscript against the final experiment matrix.
2. Confirm figures and tables map to archived outputs.
3. Review limitations and reproducibility sections for completeness.
4. Run a final submission-readiness checklist.

## Out of Scope

- Unbounded benchmark expansion during final paper drafting
- Unsupported claims not backed by benchmark artifacts

## Definition of Done

Phase 14 is complete when the benchmark paper and its reproducibility package are submission-ready.

## Completion Checklist

- [ ] Outline finalized
- [ ] Manuscript drafted
- [ ] Figures and tables integrated
- [ ] Reproducibility appendix completed
- [ ] Submission checklist reviewed
- [ ] Phase accepted and status updated in master roadmap
