# Phase 13: Paper Experiments

## Purpose

Execute the final experiment matrix needed for the benchmark paper and archive all required artifacts.

## Research Question

What experiment matrix is required to support the benchmark paper's main claims, ablations, comparisons, and limitations sections?

## Target Output

Complete paper experiment corpus with reproducible manifests and archived outputs.

## Scope

- Define final paper experiment matrix
- Execute primary comparisons
- Execute ablations and sensitivity studies
- Archive outputs and manifests
- Produce paper-ready tables and summaries

## Deliverables

- Paper experiment plan
- Experiment manifests
- Completed benchmark outputs
- Paper tables and summary artifacts
- Reproducibility package for the paper experiments

## Workstreams

- Experiment matrix design
- Execution scheduling
- Artifact archival
- Paper table preparation

## Dependencies

- Stable benchmark phases needed for the target paper scope
- Statistics and visualization outputs
- Benchmark suite definition

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The experiment matrix covers all primary claims in the paper.
2. Primary comparisons and required ablations are executed.
3. All outputs are tied to configs, metadata, and manifests.
4. Paper tables can be regenerated from stored artifacts.
5. Limitations and missing experiments are documented explicitly.

## Verification

1. Review the experiment matrix against planned paper claims.
2. Confirm all paper tables can be traced to benchmark artifacts.
3. Validate archive completeness for runs, configs, metadata, and figures.
4. Review missing or deferred experiments for disclosure quality.

## Out of Scope

- New benchmark family expansion that changes the paper scope late
- Ad hoc experiments without manifests

## Definition of Done

Phase 13 is complete when the benchmark paper's empirical backbone exists as a reproducible artifact set.

## Completion Checklist

- [ ] Experiment matrix defined
- [ ] Primary experiments executed
- [ ] Ablations executed
- [ ] Artifacts archived
- [ ] Paper tables generated
- [ ] Limitations documented
- [ ] Phase accepted and status updated in master roadmap
